BitRush

Game description

BitRush is a fast-paced first-person game with speedrunning elements. The player has to use various movement abilities and precise shooting to traverse the level and reach the end as fast as possible while avoiding obstacles and hazards. The player has a gun with limited ammo, which is used to interact with objects in the environment. Precise shots can activate or deactivate switches, mechanisms, and other elements throughout the level. The player can also use a grapple hook to swing across gaps and reach higher areas, as well as slide and dash to quickly navigate the environment and avoid obstacles.

My contribution

Grapple hook ability, Slide and dash movement abilities Mechanics of the gun, reloading etc UI elements Functionality of enemy turrets and lasers

Last Monolith

Game description

Last monoliths is a first person action game with a focus on boss battles with ancient Monoliths. The player has to use its magic and strategy in order to defeat the monoliths, using the enviroment and its abilities to overcome the challenges. The player has to complete tasks in order to make the monoliths vulnerable, and then use its magic to defeat them.

My contribution

For The Last Monolith I have acted as a lead engineer and scrum master organising the workflow, delegating tasks, making sure deadlines are met as well as assuring the code base is following up to our standards. The functionality and implementations I have done is, behaviour and AI for the end boss including its spike and charge attack pattern, all programing within the UI elements, "shieldspheres" where the player has to stand within specific zones to make them vulnerable, functionality of the pillars being destroyed, A vulnerability system where the player has to complete tasks in order to make the boss vulnerable. All transitions between stages.
